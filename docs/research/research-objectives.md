# Research Objectives

## Primary objective
Develop and evaluate a reproducible, explainable and uncertainty-aware deep-learning research system for retinal disease classification using OCT images.

## Specific objectives
1. Identify and acquire suitable OCT datasets from traceable original sources.
2. Verify dataset licences, access conditions, provenance and integrity.
3. Audit labels, metadata, image quality, duplicates and patient relationships.
4. Design patient-aware partitions and automated leakage checks.
5. Develop deterministic and documented preprocessing pipelines.
6. Establish transparent baseline models and controlled comparisons.
7. Evaluate class-wise discrimination with appropriate metrics and confidence intervals.
8. Implement and critically evaluate selected explanation methods.
9. Investigate predictive uncertainty, including epistemic and, where supportable, aleatoric components.
10. Evaluate probability calibration and selective prediction.
11. Assess robustness and independent external generalisation where suitable data are available.
12. Integrate validated research components into a tested API and dashboard.
13. Publish model cards, experiment records, evaluation reports and reproducibility instructions.

## Research outputs
- Dataset register, data dictionary and governance records.
- Versioned data manifests and split definitions.
- Reproducible preprocessing and training pipelines.
- Baseline and comparative experiment reports.
- Explainability and uncertainty evaluation reports.
- Calibration and external-validation reports, if data permit.
- Model cards and technical documentation.
- A research application with clear limitations.

## Objective acceptance
Each objective requires an associated artefact and evidence. Results will not be claimed until the relevant experiment has run, been checked and documented.
